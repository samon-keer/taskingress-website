The problem

Documents arrive everywhere. Accountability disappears.

Business documents arrive through inboxes, shared folders, manual uploads, WhatsApp messages, and external systems. The documents may be important, but the intake process is fragmented.

Teams waste time manually sorting files, chasing missing records, forwarding attachments, and checking whether someone took action. When visibility is poor, work slows down and documents fall through the cracks.

01

Files are scattered across disconnected channels.

02

Teams cannot easily see what is waiting for action.

03

Manual follow-up becomes the default workflow.
